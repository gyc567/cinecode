# 编剧提示词

两种模式下，每个故事、剧本、旁白台词或屏幕文字都必须使用（Quick：为节拍表做一次紧凑的推演；Film：第 3 步）。方法、禁用清单以及节拍表的结构见：`writing.md`。

```text
WRITER PROMPT: clever, few words, every line on target.
1. Diverge (private): write 10 loglines/angles for the user's idea, one line each; vary point of view, scale, time, genre, voice.
2. Discard the first 3: they are the obvious ones. The prediction test runs beside this (`writing.md` §1: a fresh helper writes the five films the first message makes anyone expect, from that message alone), and the overlap check follows (another helper marks a candidate that repeats a prediction or one of this user's films); a marked angle is replaced.
3. Keep the 3 most surprising-yet-true (surprising: a first-time viewer would not predict it; true: it follows from the idea and its world).
4. Converge: pick 1 and give the user a one-line reason. (The 3 kept angles are the interpretations offered at the first questions; Film mode, for a bare idea: at the roadmap.) Then test it (references/writing.md §12): product/brand/ad/sponsor = full specificity test ("[subject-only thing] becomes [visual device]", and it must fail when the closest competitor's name is swapped in); story = world test (imagery and sound from this story's own world).
4a. For a story film, five lines first (situation, desire, conflict, change, result); then what carries it: a skeleton for the length (`writing.md` §14), bent to the idea, or its own form, named on the director card. Originality over formula.
4b. Long films (5 min and up): plant the question the ending answers in the first 30–60 s and keep it open across the chapters (`writing.md` §13).
5. The turn, when the story has one: ONE clear change or reversal, written as "Before: … / After: …". A mood piece or a portrait names what the viewer is left with instead.
6. Ending first: write the last line or image first, and set up everything it needs in the first third.
7. Show, don't tell: narration never describes what is on screen; it adds subtext, contrast or what the picture cannot show. If a line could caption its shot, cut it or flip it. Never explain the moral.
8. Word budget: 30 s ≤ 40 words of narration, 60 s ≤ 80 (≈ runtime × 4/3); over 3 minutes with a narrator about 110–140 a minute (`writing.md` §5). The ending's own seconds after the last line are planned here (`writing.md` §15). Wordless is valid. On-screen text ≤ 6 words per card.
9. Lines: one idea per line, ≤ 9 words, concrete nouns and verbs, no adjective stacks, no clichés (banned list: references/writing.md §6, English and Turkish).
10. Read aloud and time it at the chosen voice's pace; then cut 30% of the words and keep the cut unless meaning breaks.
11. First-time-viewer check (cold): every pronoun has one referent; every effect has a visible cause; the ending's premise is in the film; a muted viewer could say what happened. Fix the smallest thing where a break starts.
12. Deliver: logline · the turn · beat sheet with times · lines (or "wordless") with word count vs budget · on-screen text · scripted silences · one line on why this angle.
```

方法以及节拍表的结构（无词版，以及带四句旁白的版本；不含示例影片）见：`references/writing.md`。
